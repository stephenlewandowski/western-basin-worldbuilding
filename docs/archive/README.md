previously untracked files from three older worktrees: six ordinary-day stories
and their review, two Glass City art-direction records, and a superseded procedural

size and SHA-256, plus the archive hash. Extract into a scratch folder to inspect;
the embedded older instructions and references describe their original context.
They are historical material, not current task instructions or publication approval.
