# Phase 17B — Future World Translation

Status: ACTIVE — entry / seed translation set ACCEPTED FOR DEVELOPMENT
Epistemic scope: S (scenario) and K (working sketch) only
Primary horizon: 2075, with 2026 evidence baseline and 2050 transition

## Objective

Turn accepted system and scenario results into observable, lived future conditions that can be seen, heard, maintained, governed, repaired, inhabited, argued about, relied upon, or failed by in the 2075 Western Basin.

This is scenario translation, not new scientific modeling. The governing grammar is:

```text
REAL PLACE → SYSTEM → FUTURE CONDITION → HUMAN EXPERIENCE → ARTIFACT → UNCERTAINTY
```

Evidence constrains scenario. Scenario may inform later canon. No Phase 17B seed condition is canon merely because it is written down.

## Translation unit

Each seed unit should record, where supported:

- ID and macroregion;
- relevant Atlas system IDs;
- 2026 evidence anchor;
- scenario driver and A/B/C envelope;
- 2050 transition;
- 2075 future condition;
- what persists, what is modified, and what is added;
- operational practice;
- governance or institutional implication;
- maintenance / repair signature;
- human experience;
- visible or material artifact and, when useful, a sensory cue;
- failure or stress expression;
- technology interface;
- uncertainty;
- explicit epistemic status (`E`, `S`, `C`, or `K`).

The Phase 17B seed matrix uses `S` and `K` only. `E` appears only as an evidence anchor, never as the status of a future condition. Earlier accepted Phase 1–13 products may provide place- or system-specific evidence anchors. Phase 17A S/K prototypes may be reused only as prior translation precedents; acceptance as a prototype does not promote scenario or sketch content to evidence. `C` is prohibited unless a fictional condition was already canon before this transaction; none is used here.

## Temporal rule

- **2026** is the evidence baseline: accepted current systems, dependencies, observations, assets, and boundaries.
- **2050** is a transition or partial-adaptation horizon: additions are uneven, incomplete, maintained, negotiated, or still under repair.
- **2075** is the primary lived future horizon: accumulated futures are shown as old and new infrastructure occupying the same place.

Do not use a clean-sheet replacement, a precise adoption date, a capacity claim, or a future performance claim unsupported by the accepted repository.

## Scenario rule

Regimes A, B, and C are envelopes of institutional and technological conditions, not competing predictions:

- **A — Coordinated Technological Adaptation:** selected interoperable interfaces, auditable stewardship, maintenance and human review, with residual cost and inequality.
- **B — Uneven Networked Modernization:** strong and weak nodes connected through negotiated adapters, mixed standards, uneven access, and differentiated maintenance.
- **C — High Capability / High Friction Basin:** substantial capability at selected nodes alongside brittle dependencies, contested legitimacy, cyber/digital exposure, and delayed or fragmented oversight.

The matrix labels a unit's primary envelope reading as `ROBUST ACROSS A/B/C`, `REGIME-SENSITIVE`, or `UNRESOLVED`. `ROBUST ACROSS A/B/C` describes persistence of the translation proposition across scenario envelopes. It is not a resilience/robustness score or a claim that the underlying system is robust. These are qualitative translation dispositions, not scores, rankings, or probabilities.

## Human-scale rule

Every seed should eventually support at least one concrete bridge to Phase 17C without beginning Phase 17C here:

- a maintenance action;
- an operational routine;
- a household or workplace practice;
- a public-space condition;
- an infrastructure artifact;
- a governance interaction;
- a visible ecological condition; or
- an information/data interaction.

The first seed set deliberately emphasizes utility rooms, drainage, fields, working waterfronts, industrial yards, public notices, inspection equipment, manual fallback, repair access, and institutional interfaces rather than characters.

## Production and review boundary

1. Use accepted repository material from the Phase 1–16 foundation, with the Phase 14 ontology/dependency layer, Phase 15 technology and A/B/C scenario layer, Phase 16A propagation framework, and accepted Phase 16B compound-stress package as the main translation basis.
2. Translate qualitatively; do not calculate new quantities, probabilities, rankings, forecasts, or scores.
3. Keep contamination, exposure, dose, illness; surveillance signal, incidence; mobility, transmission; dependency, failure; propagation, probability; and response option, successful response separate.
4. Keep AI advisory, observation distinct from control, and technology distinct from automatic protection.
5. Keep circular exchanges open: residual is not automatically a recoverable or qualified input.
6. Do not use the held Great Black Swamp candidate geometry. Black Swamp Country may be used as an interpretive macroregion, not as a newly adopted polygon.
7. Do not create characters, canon, Phase 17C material, polished Atlas spreads, animation, tags, or releases.

The deliverables are the seed matrix and a small set of human-review condition cards. They are scenario-development material, not canon.
