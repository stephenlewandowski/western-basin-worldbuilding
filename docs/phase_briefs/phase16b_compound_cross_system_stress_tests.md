# Phase 16B — Compound Cross-System Stress Tests

Status: APPROVED SCOPE / NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Phase 16B is deferred. Its future scope may select and execute a bounded set of compound stress-test chains using the Phase 16A vocabulary, propagation rules, response mechanisms, technology modifiers, and chain template.

Candidate tests may include combinations of extreme heat, low flow, grid stress, water-quality pressure, treatment disruption, freight/material disruption, industrial energy constraint, infectious-disease pressure, surveillance/data-governance friction, heavy precipitation/flooding, logistics disruption, cyber/digital-resilience stress, and sensor/decision-support degradation.

Phase 16B must not be implemented by Phase 16A. It must remain high-level for infectious disease and biosecurity, must not introduce operational attack or pathogen detail, and must not convert qualitative pathways into risk, probability, resilience, vulnerability, or health-outcome models.
