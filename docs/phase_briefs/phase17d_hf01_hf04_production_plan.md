# Phase 17D — HF-01 / HF-04 production plan

**Scope:** revise HF-01 Glass City into a public hero set, then produce HF-04 Industrial Metabolism as the approved second hero **after HF-01 human review**. ## HF-01 — Glass City 2075

| Criterion | Direction / review test |
| --- | --- |
| Place identity | A Toledo-derived Maumee working street: retained brick/concrete shells, loading rhythm, river light and freight edge. A composite view must feel specific without claiming a real block. |
| Technological convergence | A *deep, branching* optical canopy reorganizes shade, public circulation, freight handoff and service access. Distinguish shade/optics from separate power, compute/cooling and mechanical support. Its silhouette must announce a changed urban section at thumbnail size. |
| Maintenance visibility | Make a cassette replacement the focal action: empty berth, lifting path, exposed joint, cordon and glaziers. The detail view moves closer to the human-guided swap and worn connectors; the inset separates old wall, attachment, service rib, glazing and access. |
| Atmosphere | Lower the viewpoint toward a pedestrian route; stage humid post-rain daylight, warm/cool contrast, wet ground and recognizable river-facing depth. Keep the action readable through the weather. |
| Material language | Clear, patterned, cloudy and replaced panels must transmit or filter light differently; aged brick, patched paving and steel provide friction. Avoid one silver canopy material. |
| Ordinary human use | A person crosses into shade, another waits near a public room, while deliveries and maintenance continue. Property discontinuity and a closed bay show access is negotiated. |
| Avoid | Generic glass tower, abstract canopy floating above the street, visual claims of cooling effectiveness, seamless robotic work, a real waterfront project, or a near-identical rerender of the current study. |

## HF-04 — Industrial Metabolism 2075

| Criterion | Direction / review test |
| --- | --- |
| Place identity | A synthetic Toledo/Great Lakes industrial precinct with retained masonry crane hall, concrete yard, rail/service freight and adjacent work. Do not assign a new process to a named plant. |
| Technological convergence | The old hall contains a tall modular exchange frontage: protected optical qualification gallery, removable manufacturing cells, bounded robotic handoff, separated material bays and accessible heat/water interfaces. The open end should reveal this vertical organization in one wide frame. |
| Maintenance visibility | Old crane lowers a large replacement cell toward an empty berth; riggers and a technician supervise isolation. The detail view shows lifting, worn liner/seal or dusty optical screen and a staffed acceptance point. The inset sections a cell, qualification gate, reject route, utilities and outside flow. |
| Atmosphere | Daylight cuts through dusty hall air onto the gallery and suspended cell; cooler yard light and freight beyond show an open industrial system. Use haze for depth, not to disguise missing connections. |
| Material language | Soot-dark brick, scarred mounting points, heavy steel, protected glass, labeled segregated stores and replaceable opaque equipment; avoid glowing mystery machinery. |
| Ordinary human use | Quality worker rejects an ambiguous batch, riggers guide the lift, a dispatcher handles freight, and a person authorizes handoff. These actions must read separately from automation. |
| Avoid | Universal recycling machine, every residual becoming stock, invisible rejection or external inputs, closed-loop/zero-waste imagery, throughput or recovery claims, and a pristine automated factory. |

## Sequence and gates

1. **HF-01 composition first.** Use the existing prototype lineage to make one low-resolution camera/silhouette and light study. Human review decides whether the canopy, Toledo cues, public route and repair action are compelling at home-page thumbnail size. Resolve that decision before a full-resolution hero render.
2. **HF-01 finish.** Produce the wide image, inspect it at site width and full size, then make the distinct maintenance view and revise the SVG inset to match the selected design. Human review checks visual impact, material truth, ordinary use, caption/alt text and E/S/K boundaries. Treat accepted HF-01 as the calibration for HF-04.
3. **HF-04 after that gate.** Make one low-resolution hall composition and section logic. Review whether the lifted cell, qualification/rejection and outside flows read at once. Only then finish the wide, maintenance view and SVG, followed by the same human review.
4. **Delivery.** Keep one optimized WebP per raster view and one lean SVG per inset. Start with target budgets of about 1 MiB per wide, 600 KiB per detail and 50 KiB per SVG; inspect legibility before lowering quality, and record justified exceptions. Do not retain duplicate full-size PNG/JPEG/AVIF deliveries or bulky intermediate renders. Preserve scripts, key settings and caption/source notes for revision.

Hermes can execute low-resolution studies, reproducible builders, compression, file naming and provenance notes, then integrate approved assets into the site. A human selects the composition and accepts each hero and its claims before public publication.
