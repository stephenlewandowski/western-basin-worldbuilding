# Glass Basin invention catalog v0.1

**Phase 17D working catalog · 2026-09-28 · S/K only · no new canon**

An *invention* here is a reusable visual and architectural proposition, not a patented device, selected future, approved project, or performance claim. “Need” names a conditional scenario problem. E remains inherited context; S supplies conditional system relationships; the described form and deployment are K. The first two entries are distilled from the published [HF-01](../../reports/phase17d_hf01_final_production_notes.md) and [HF-04](../../reports/phase17d_hf04_final_production_notes.md) sets, the accepted [Phase 17D concepts](../../reports/phase17d_hero_futurescape_concepts.md), and the [two-hero retrospective](../../reports/phase17d_two_hero_visual_retrospective.md).

## GBI-01 — Optical civic canopy / replaceable leaf system

**Source:** HF-01 Glass City 2075. A family of directional leaves forms a deep, maintained public roof over older urban fabric; it is not a continuous citywide dome.

| Field | Working definition |
| --- | --- |
| Need | Conditional heat, shade, daylight, public passage, and service needs where existing streets and buildings have uneven access and ownership. No cooling or health effect is established. |
| Function | Filter or admit daylight, provide a serviceable shade envelope, and coordinate distinct public, freight, power, data, and repair routes. Optical functions do not supply unlimited energy or erase cooling loads. |
| Physical form | Directional leaf families with thick replaceable cassette edges, repeatable attachment shoes, an open berth, and walkable service ribs tied to legacy structure or rail where suitable. Disconnected bays remain possible. |
| Convergence stack | Glass/optical materials and sensing, mechanical mounting and lift access, separately routed electrical and data services, bounded freight handling, local advisory control, and human operating authority. Each interface needs space. |
| Human interface | Passersby, people waiting or using a public room, glaziers, delivery workers, building staff, and authorized operators. Access and closure decisions stay with people and institutions. |
| Maintenance / failure modes | Dirty or delaminated glazing, worn seals, sticking actuators, failed connectors, misaligned shoes, obsolete electronics, and incompatible panel generations. Replacement needs cordon, lift, inspection, and a safe service route; the image is not a work instruction. |
| Visual signature | Branching translucent leaves over an inhabited route; one conspicuous repair or missing panel; differing clear, patterned, cloudy, and replacement surfaces filtering real light. |
| Recurring materials | Layered and patterned glass, gasketed cassette edges, steel ribs and mounts, separate service conduits, patched paving, hand-worn public furnishings, and an aged host structure. Old brick is contextual, not required. |
| S/K boundary | E: inherited Toledo/Western Basin settlement, industry, weather, and systems context. S: conditional adaptation and dependence on labor, energy, access, and governance. K: leaf geometry, attachment, street layout, and depicted operation. No structural suitability, optical output, bird safety, affordability, citywide adoption, or new C is asserted. |
| Plausible settings beyond the original image | Other **composite** working streets, transit or freight thresholds, and public-room forecourts where the host structure and access boundary differ. None implies a real parcel or automatic cross-property continuity. |
| Decorative failure test | If leaves can be deleted without changing passage, light, maintenance access, or authority—or if mounts, service space, panel differences, and an operating boundary disappear—the canopy is decorative glass scenery. |

## GBI-02 — Qualified modular industrial process cell

**Source:** HF-04 Industrial Metabolism 2075. The cell is one replaceable member of a staffed qualification and exchange *system*, not a universal residual-to-product machine.

| Field | Working definition |
| --- | --- |
| Need | Conditional adaptation of production to variable materials while keeping characterization, rejection, contracts, utilities, and external outlets visible. An available residual is not automatically a usable input. |
| Function | Isolate and replace a process module; characterize and route material through a bounded acceptance decision; preserve separate incoming, held/rejected, qualified, and outgoing dispositions. No successful exchange is guaranteed. |
| Physical form | Opaque serviceable cell with lift points and worn connections; an empty berth and access clearance; protected optical qualification gallery; segregated bays; accessible heat/water interfaces; a bounded robotic handoff and outside freight edge. Different processes need different equipment. |
| Convergence stack | Photonic metrology, human-reviewed quality data and advisory compute, advanced manufacturing, robotic handling, physical materials segregation, utility service, freight, and institutional acceptance. Sensing does not itself certify compatibility. |
| Human interface | Riggers supervise replacement; a technician checks isolation; a quality worker holds or rejects ambiguity; a dispatcher manages external movement; an authorized person accepts handoff. These are distinct decisions and work roles. |
| Maintenance / failure modes | Worn liners and seals, dusty or drifting optics, connection mismatch, thermal wear, robot faults, software obsolescence, unavailable replacement parts, and a batch that stays held or leaves as residual. Repair costs materials, labor, time, and freight. |
| Visual signature | Tall modular frontage with one empty berth and one opaque cell in service; a protected, staffed gallery; physically separate material bays and visible outward freight. A crane is one possible service means, not the identity of every use. |
| Recurring materials | Heavy steel frames and mounts, opaque equipment casings, protected and sacrificial glass, accessible pipes/valves, marked containers, scarred concrete, and aging host fabric. Brick and a historic crane belong to HF-04's image, not the portable requirement. |
| S/K boundary | E: inherited Toledo/Great Lakes manufacturing, materials, and freight context. S: FT-03's conditional exchange, qualification, outside dependence, and responsibility. K: cell family, hall arrangement, specific interface, and depicted work. No named plant, compatible stream, throughput, recovery fraction, environmental result, or new C is asserted. |
| Plausible settings beyond the original image | Other **synthetic** freight-adjacent production, repair, or materials-receiving interiors with their own qualification and service arrangement. Transferring the motif never assigns a process to a real facility. |
| Decorative failure test | If a box glows while unlabeled mixed material flows in and useful stock flows out—without hold/reject space, utilities, a human acceptance point, service access, or outside freight—it is decorative automation, not this invention. |
