# Basin Reading Hall — orientation A continuity lock

**User selection · 3 October 2026.**
[Composition A](../../assets/phase17d/hf05/hf05_composition_a.webp) is the canonical
visual orientation for this place. This lock fixes relative zones and screen
handedness for subsequent scenes; it does not accept every depicted fitting,
person or engineering detail. Front/rear and left/right are design directions,
not geographic bearings. No measured dimensions or real parcel are established.

## The view to preserve

[Continuity plan](../../assets/phase17d/hf05/hf05_orientation_a_continuity.svg)
shows the relationships in one editable drawing.

From the public side of the counter, the counter occupies the **foreground left**.
An open reading floor fills the middle. The instrument edge runs along the
**right**, rising from the low left/deeper arrival end through a middle tier to
the tall right/nearer end. Public visitors stay on the reading floor.

| Zone | Locked relation |

| Public counter and edition cabinet | Foreground-left reading edge; visitor on public side, steward behind the timber counter. Public current/prior editions remain here, with private conversation space nearby. |

The public request, sealed sample handoff and isolated equipment connection are
three separate crossings. A conversation may cross a rail or counter; the person
does not thereby cross its access boundary. The comparison dock remains within
the staff method workspace, reachable from the staff corridor and rear service route.

## Refinement of the earlier sketch

The [original spatial study](../../assets/phase17d/hf05/hf05_basin_reading_hall_spatial_study.svg)
placed the exchange widening closer to the low/middle end. This lock relocates
that **single ground berth to the right/tall end** to match A. Arrival, counter
and public route stay in their existing conceptual relations. The original
[production brief](phase17d_hf05_basin_reading_hall_production_brief.md) and study
remain preserved as preceding design records; this lock takes precedence for
scene continuity where their berth placement differs.

A’s large opaque block behind the left/low end remains unassigned retained fabric;
it is not the compute/preservation wing. A’s front-facing upper landings are staff
access, not the rear service gallery and not public walkways. B and C remain
camera studies, not alternate floor plans.

## Scene continuity and withdrawn comic intent

The strip failed human-author continuity review and is withdrawn. Its intended
panel blocking is not accepted narrative continuity. Subsequent scenes must keep
the public encounter at the same counter, approach the left return hatch from the
public floor, and leave the ground berth behind the staff boundary. Detail crops
may change camera angle without mirroring the hall or moving its zones.

The generated figures in A are not Renata/Hannah portrait references. The withdrawn
strip does not establish character or event continuity. Institutional rules, biographies and
technology performance remain provisional fiction/design. The only canon decision
here is the user-selected visual orientation and its qualitative continuity relations.
