# Phase 11C — Population & Settlement Futures, 2050 / 2075

Status: APPROVED SCOPE / NOT IMPLEMENTED.

## Approved scope only

Phase 11C may later examine qualitative alternative population and settlement futures for 2050 and 2075 over the accepted Phase 11A/11B 2026 factual baselines. It may represent divergence in population distribution, settlement concentration/deconcentration, housing form, employment geography, mobility interfaces, infrastructure-service dependence, and urban/suburban/rural form through explicit scenario assumptions and separate future-state records.

Future records must remain separate from factual 2026 tables and carry scenario identifiers, horizon, assumption/basis, reality status, canon status, uncertainty, and relationship basis. A 2050 or 2075 scenario is not a forecast unless an approved evidence-backed forecasting method is later authorized.

## Required boundaries

No unsupported demographic forecast, probability, precise future population total, individual/household profile, protected-class ranking, social-vulnerability/EJ score, community-worth ranking, migration-causation claim, individual movement model, utility-territory assignment, health outcome, disease/vector module, Indigenous-history/HGIS layer, or automatic scenario-to-fact promotion.

Phase 11C must preserve Great Black Swamp `C — HOLD / noncanonical` and the unresolved Toledo intake-coordinate discrepancy. It must not modify Phase 11A/11B factual baselines, Phase 1–10 accepted/frozen artifacts, or current canon.

## Not implemented in this session

No Phase 11C scenario tables, maps, forecasts, assumptions, or future population values are created by this package. A future session requires a new approved implementation brief and transition gate after formal Sol review of Phase 11A/11B.
