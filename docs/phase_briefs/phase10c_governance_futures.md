# Phase 10C — Governance Futures, 2050 / 2075

Status: ACCEPTED / FROZEN by Sol explicit acceptance decision on 2026-09-07.

## Scope implemented

Phase 10C creates separate qualitative 2050/2075 governance-futures scenario layers over the accepted/frozen Phase 10A/10B 2026 institutional baselines. It models three distinct alternatives: A — Integrated Basin Governance; B — Federated / Networked Governance; and C — Fragmented / Contested Governance.

The package examines coordination, information sharing, authority interfaces, resilience finance, cross-system dependencies, binational and tribal participation, public/private coordination, ecological-infrastructure governance, adaptive management, emergency coordination, and bounded technology/governance interfaces. It does not predict exact future law, elections, partisan control, collapse, governance quality, or deterministic institutional behavior.

All future records distinguish CURRENT FACT (2026 baseline), SCENARIO ASSUMPTION, and SCENARIO CONSEQUENCE. Future authority changes are scenario assumptions, not legal predictions. Integration does not mean centralization. Scenario B is a distinct networked-pluralism future rather than a midpoint.
