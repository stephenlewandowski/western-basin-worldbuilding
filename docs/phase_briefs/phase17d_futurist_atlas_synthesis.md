# Phase 17D — Futurist Atlas Synthesis

## Purpose

Restore futurism and technology convergence to the headline Atlas while using the scientific backbone to constrain claims. Phase 17D translates inherited evidence and accepted scenario relationships into five architectural futurescape concepts. The concepts are S/K development material: they propose spatial possibilities without asserting approved projects, simultaneous deployment, engineering readiness, or a selected future. No new canon (C) is created.

The working synthesis is [Phase 17D hero futurescape concepts](../../reports/phase17d_hero_futurescape_concepts.md). Preserve its five concepts and detailed uncertainty boundaries during production.

## Presentation hierarchy

1. **HERO FUTURESCAPE** — the place and its large-scale physical transformation.
2. **SYSTEM EXPLANATION** — a small number of annotations and insets that explain consequential interfaces, dependencies, labor, and access.
3. **EVIDENCE / PROTOCOL** — sources, methods, status boundaries, and validation that let readers trace why the interpretation is bounded.

Protocol sheets remain valuable, but generally serve as supporting or drill-down products rather than headline visuals. An opening image should raise a clear question; its system explanation and evidence should make the answer inspectable.

## Design rule

**Scientific restraint governs claims, not visual conservatism.** A future scene may substantially reorganize architecture, work, and public space while remaining conditional S/K material. Futurism must come from technology changing physical form, service relationships, and daily activity—not from decorative devices or unexplained glowing effects. Maintenance, degradation, energy and material needs, labor, unequal access, governance, and uncertainty remain visible.

Each hero pairs a legible base scene with an optional high-convergence K variant when a more ambitious configuration helps explore consequential technology convergence. A variant remains K, is not a forecast or preferred future, and need not appear in the base image. It still obeys maintenance, energy, material, labor, access, governance, and uncertainty constraints. It should test consequential convergence rather than add decorative gadgets.

## Temporal framework

- **2026 — evidence/model anchor:** inherited evidence, observed assets, and validated scientific products establish the present reference. The concept work does not reopen those models or invent measured site geometry.
- **2050 — partial/uneven transition:** selected interfaces, repairs, and institutions may change at different rates. Retained structures and gaps remain.
- **2075 — primary lived future:** show the inhabited place after conditional changes have accumulated, including obsolete equipment, incomplete connections, maintenance work, and contested access.

These are interpretive horizons, not dates for promised deployments. Transition passages do not invent intervening events or construction schedules.

## Epistemic framework

- **E = evidence/model:** inherited observations, sources, and validated model results.
- **S = scenario:** conditional system relationships and futures inherited from accepted scenario work.
- **K = sketch:** new architectural arrangements, interfaces, and optional technology combinations proposed for exploration.
- **C = canon:** formally established world facts.

No new C is created in Phase 17D concept work. Scenario regime C is not canon C. Real-place anchors guide interpretation but do not imply surveyed viewpoints, parcels, or proposed construction.

## Technology convergence

Explore combinations across AI and advanced compute; robotics and autonomy; photonics and optics; advanced sensing; application-specific quantum-derived technologies; advanced energy and storage; fusion where scenario-appropriate; advanced manufacturing; advanced materials; biotechnology; molecular monitoring; and cyber, privacy, and identity. Select combinations for what they change in architecture, service access, work, and public life. Compute requires power, cooling, mass, and replacement access. Automated systems require recovery, skilled labor, and authority. Biology carries containment, qualification, monitoring, and governance obligations. Sensing supports interpretation but does not prove performance. Quantum-derived functions require application-specific comparison; no ubiquitous advantage is assumed. Fusion is optional K where appropriate, without a deployment claim.

## Glass Basin visual grammar

Use functional translucency that admits, filters, protects, samples, or enables inspection; old and new structures layered so their joints and load questions remain visible; replaceable components with seams and accessible service routes; active Great Lakes weather that shapes use; computation accompanied by physical mass, power, cooling, and replacement space; biology with containment, maintenance, and governance obligations; repair and maintenance as ordinary work; public and work interfaces with visible access boundaries; and specific evidence of incompleteness such as a disconnected bay, rejected material, empty berth, or mismatched replacement panel. Avoid seamless perfection and decorative data effects.

Shared materials and maintenance language establish family resemblance. Preserve distinct silhouettes, sections, landscape relationships, and primary activities: HF-01’s broad optical canopy and inhabited urban arcade; HF-02’s horizontal lake-edge service terrace and recovery cradle; HF-03’s long folded bio-ag cultivation/processing seam; HF-04’s tall modular interior inside an old crane hall; and HF-05’s stacked instrument wall, service voids, and civic observation hall.

## Hero set

- **HF-01 — Glass City 2075:** Toledo-derived optical urban arcade, public use, freight handoff, and repair around retained industrial shells.
- **HF-02 — Lake Erie Energy & Security Coast:** weather-exposed service terrace for energy, lake observation, recovery, and accountable access.
- **HF-03 — Maumee Bio-Ag Landscape:** a working field edge where cultivation, biological processing, robotics, drainage, and stewardship share a maintained seam.
- **HF-04 — Industrial Metabolism 2075:** modular industrial exchange and qualification inside a retained crane hall, with rejection and external flows visible.
- **HF-05 — Western Basin Information Ecology:** a civic observation hall joining field samples, instruments, interpretation, privacy, and human authority.

All five remain composite futurescape concepts, not project proposals or geographic reconstructions. The Great Black Swamp hold remains in force; no held geometry is introduced.

## High-convergence variants

Variants remain optional K explorations separate from each base hero image. HF-02 may test a fusion-era coastal energy interface through conversion, storage, grid coupling, thermal management, maintenance, access, accountability, and industrial form; no reactor type, output, date, or certainty is specified. HF-03 may test a more ambitious contained biological-processing interface while retaining qualification, conventional farming, external inputs and outputs, and no environmental-release assumption; monitoring does not prove performance. HF-05 may test one application-specific quantum-derived measurement suite against established methods while retaining calibration, environmental control, maintenance, specialist labor, and interpretation; there is no general-purpose truth detector or ubiquitous advantage claim. No symmetry-driven variants are required for HF-01 or HF-04.

## Production sequence

1. **HF-01 — Glass City 2075:** first visual production prototype.
2. **HF-04 — Industrial Metabolism 2075:** likely second prototype, subject to HF-01 review.
3. **HF-02, HF-03, and HF-05:** proceed after visual review of the first prototypes.

This is a production sequence, not a ranking of futures. HF-01 is the next approved production experiment; visual production begins as a separate follow-on task after this synthesis is integrated.

## Storage policy

- **Master:** retain reproducible Blender, SVG, Python, or documented illustration source as appropriate to each product.
- **Delivery:** provide optimized WebP or AVIF and SVG where each format serves a purpose.
- **Avoid:** giant redundant raster masters, duplicate delivery formats without a delivery benefit, and animation without unique explanatory value.

Retain source geometry, scripts, annotations, materials, prompts, references, and composition instructions needed to reproduce or revise the work. Keep analytical insets legible and distinct from quantitative scientific maps.
