# Phase 13B — Infectious Disease Dependencies, Surveillance Gaps & Response Interfaces, 2026

Status: APPROVED SCOPE / NOT IMPLEMENTED.

This future phase may add a separate qualitative dependency layer over the accepted Phase 13A baseline. It may examine climate/vector, hydrology/water, food systems, settlement/mobility, healthcare/institutional, laboratory/reporting, governance, and public-information interfaces using explicit documented-versus-inferred relationship fields.

It must preserve the distinctions between pathogen presence, exposure, infection, reported case, local transmission, outbreak, and disease burden; surveillance intensity and incidence; residence and exposure location; non-reporting and pathogen absence; and county surveillance and local risk. It may include a qualitative surveillance-gap or response-interface register, but not a vulnerability score, county danger ranking, neighborhood ranking, outbreak probability, or composite disease-risk index.

Phase 13B must use Phase 13A as an immutable factual working baseline, keep all relationships qualitative unless directly supported, preserve the Phase 1–12 freeze boundary and active holds, and receive a separate approval before implementation. No Phase 13B product is implemented by Phase 13A.
