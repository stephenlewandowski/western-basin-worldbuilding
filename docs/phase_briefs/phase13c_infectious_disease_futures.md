# Phase 13C — Infectious Disease System Futures, 2050 / 2075

Status: APPROVED SCOPE / NOT IMPLEMENTED.

This future phase may develop separate qualitative A/B/C scenario layers for infectious-disease surveillance, environmental interfaces, population/settlement context, vector ecology, institutional response, and information systems at 2050 and 2075. Future objects must remain separate from factual 2026 Phase 13A records and must carry explicit scenario assumptions, horizon, reality status, provenance/basis, uncertainty, and relationship status.

It must not present ecological suitability as disease burden, case counts as transmission rates, surveillance intensity as incidence, residence as exposure location, non-reporting as absence, contamination as illness, positive vectors as human infection, or historical pandemic behavior as a future baseline. It may not create a disease-risk score, vulnerability/EJ score, outbreak forecast, exact future incidence, individual risk surface, unsupported local downscaling, or composite score across unlike diseases.

Phase 13C must preserve Phase 13A and all accepted/frozen prior artifacts as immutable baselines, preserve Great Black Swamp `C — HOLD / noncanonical` and the Toledo intake-coordinate discrepancy as unresolved, and receive separate approval before implementation. No Phase 13C product is implemented by Phase 13A.
