# Erie Drift: places, fishing lore and species

Consulted 5 October 2026. This is the source record for a fictional 2075 browser
game, not a fishing forecast, navigation chart or stock assessment.

Six grounds use familiar names: Maumee Bay, Toledo Intake, Niagara Reef, South
Bass, Kelleys Island and East Harbor. Positions and shore outlines are compressed,
depths are authored, and dotted lines are budget connections rather than navigable
courses. The intake is a distant landmark, not a docking destination. Existing
Atlas geography and its source coordinates are not changed.

## Fish and tackle

- [Ohio DNR, Lake Erie Fishing Guide](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/ohiodnr.gov/documents/wildlife/fishing/Pub5276_LakeErieFishingGuide_Web.pdf):
  perch near the bottom, walleye presentations, and smallmouth around rock with
  jigs/soft plastics. Historical regulations in this guide are not used as current
  or future rules.
- [Ohio DNR, Sport Fish of Ohio](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/ohiodnr.gov/documents/wildlife/backyard-wildlife/Sport%20Fish%20of%20Ohio%20Field%20Guide%20pub334.pdf):
  species and habitat context. Bluegill are assigned to sheltered bay habitat,
  rather than treated as equally likely offshore. Freshwater drum carries the
  familiar local name **sheepshead**.
- [USGS/NOAA GLANSIS: white perch](https://nas.er.usgs.gov/queries/greatlakes/FactSheet.aspx?Species_ID=777):
  established nonnative Great Lakes fish, with Lake Erie records. White perch is
  distinct from native white bass.
- [USGS: the round goby connection in Lake Erie](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/a-potential-new-energy-pathway-central-lake-erie-round-goby-connection):
  Lake Erie round goby presence and food-web context. The game's encounter rate
  does not reproduce the study's sampling or conclusions.
- [USGS/NOAA GLANSIS: northern snakehead, potential invader](https://nas.er.usgs.gov/queries/greatLakes/FactSheet.aspx?HUCNumber=DGreatLakes&Potential=Y&Species_ID=2265&Type=2):
  the source discusses potential Great Lakes invasion, not an established Lake
  Erie population. Snakehead appears only with an explicitly enabled fictional
  2075 introduction scenario, and only in the game's bay habitat. Off by default.

Minnows, nightcrawlers and soft-plastic tubes change authored catch weights.
Bottom spreader, drift jig, spinner harness and light float have different bait
compatibilities and fishing depths. None guarantees the target species.
Lengths, bite rates, fighting rhythms and scores are invented for play; none is
an empirical distribution or a fishery projection. Solar response uses the broad
principle that sunlight varies with cloud and season, without a calibrated panel,
weather or boat-performance model.

Invasive catches use **Log encounter for dock review** instead of a release or
supper choice. This is a narrative abstraction, not instructions for real fish
handling. Logged, released and kept fish all count toward the journal score.
The three-fish basket is a household/game limit, not a regulation or consumption
advisory. Real-world restrictions and advice are outside the game.
