# Research Documents

Research notes belong here with dates, sources, uncertainty, and scope. Indigenous historical geography must be dated and sourced rather than flattened into timeless territory polygons.

- [Indigenous Peoples' History](Glasspunk_Indigenous_Peoples_History.md) is preserved as a separate research module.
- [Great Black Swamp History](Great_Black_Swamp_History.md) is preserved byte-for-byte as contextual deep-history research. It does not supersede the separate geometry QA review or authorize a canonical polygon.
- Living sovereign descendant nations must not be replaced with fictional generic tribes.
- Archaeological coordinates may require generalization for protection.
- Lower Maumee / Ottawa history is a priority future historical GIS layer; reconstruction has not begun.
