# Concept Maps

Files in this area are brainstorming and visual-development artifacts. They are **not geographic sources** and must not be used to derive coordinates, boundaries, infrastructure locations, or other analytical geometry.

Archived generated maps can contain known errors, including:

- Genoa misplaced in an earlier map
- Put-in-Bay confused with island geography; Put-in-Bay is the community on South Bass Island
- Catawba treated as an island rather than a peninsula/mainland location
- inaccurate treatment of Kelleys Island or the Bass Islands
- an early inaccurate Toledo intake placement
- use of the superseded six-region model

Authoritative geography comes from the Python/R analytical atlas, its cited public datasets, and documented QA. The archive is retained to preserve the project’s visual-development history.
