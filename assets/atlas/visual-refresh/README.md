# Illustrated regions and architectural sketches

Reader-facing concept art made on 4 October 2026, following the author's request
for a fantasy regional map, blueprint-style equipment and readable study content.
These files replace prominent diagram displays; they do not replace scientific
products, original drawings or source records.

| Asset | Reader use | Grounding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `illustrated_regions_2075.webp` | Maps and homepage | Five regional identities; retained geographic derivative; earlier sketchbook style |
| `canopy_blueprint.webp` | Glass City equipment | Retained wall, replaceable cassette, service rib, separate services and property gap |
| `industry_blueprint.webp` | Crane-hall equipment | Removable cell, empty berth, qualification workspace, separated material bays and outside freight |
| `farm_blueprint.webp` | Agricultural equipment | Open cultivation, selective propagation, repair, contained work, gate/ditch/wetland and outside supply |
| `coast_blueprint.webp` | Coastal equipment | Access threshold, utility isolation, comparison bench, dry civilian-survey recovery, quay and lake |
| `crib_blueprint.webp` | Intake crib study | Paired approximate existing body and possible future equipment |

[Manifest](manifest.json) records full prompts, reference paths/hashes, original
PNG hashes and WebP packaging. [Original PNG archive](source_pngs.zip) preserves
all six generated masters. Export uses Sharp WebP quality 88, effort 6, original
resolution and no semantic image edits. Extract a master and apply those settings
to reproduce the packaged derivative. Generation itself is not deterministic.

The map's shore detail, architecture and distances are stylized. It supplies no
territorial boundary, surveyed future siting or historical swamp polygon. The
real-place explorer retains its independent sourced locations and physical
geography. The fantasy map does not establish new fictional canon.

The equipment sketches use approximate perspective and inferred geometry. Their
six numbers refer to adjacent authored descriptions, not certified engineering.
No dimensions, performance, throughput, water treatment or successful calibration
are established. The crib's physical location is verified independently; its
sketch geometry is not measured. No third-party crib photograph was used.

Earlier drawings remain in [the futurescape asset collections](../../phase17d/)
and [original study plates](../../../outputs/atlas/prototypes/). Their scientific
and creative decision history is preserved. The withdrawn comic remains withdrawn.

Source render references are project-owned generated artwork and retained project
plates. Research data and third-party material keep their original terms; see
[asset licensing](../../../docs/references/DATA_AND_ASSET_LICENSING.md).
