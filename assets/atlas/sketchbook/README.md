# Original sketchbook web derivatives

Prepared 6 October 2026 from the three preserved PNG sheets in
[concept_art](../../concept_art/README.md). Nine curated crops and three full-sheet
WebP copies support the public Sketchbook collection. No original image, label,
map, character name or annotation was altered. No new image was generated.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) records source hashes, original dimensions, crop
rectangles and derivative hashes. The source sheets are 1536 × 1024 pixels.
Packaging script used locally: `temp/sketchbook-integration/package-sketchbook.cjs`.
It performs Sharp extraction and WebP compression only; derivatives can be rebuilt
from the retained source PNGs and the manifest's crop rectangles and quality
settings (86 for sheets, 88 for crops).

The collection preserves early generated concept art. Embedded technology claims,
dates, names and regional maps are exploratory history. Current five-region
navigation places the former Frontier Arc's agricultural/maker themes alongside
wetland themes in Black Swamp Country. Original six-region maps are shown in their
historical context, not reused as current geography.

Characters are presented as role and clothing studies, without assigning
biographies or equating them with current story characters. Names and occupations
vary between sheets. Source depictions establish no technology performance,
future deployment, historical swamp boundary or new fictional canon.
