# Biogeochemical & Nutrient Flux Sources

The Phase 8 module uses the machine-readable registry at `data/processed/networks/biogeochemical_system_sources.csv`. US EPA Lake Erie data document annual phosphorus-load tracking, western-basin eutrophic context, Maumee importance, and target/status distinctions. EPA's Maumee Watershed Nutrient TMDL approval documents a planning framework allocating western-basin phosphorus between point and nonpoint sources. USGS hydrography/WBD, NWI, NCWQR, USDA, Ohio EPA, H2Ohio, GLRI Annex 4, and NOAA provide bounded system and management context.

No source is used to infer farm-level behavior, a continuous nutrient route, a basin-wide station measurement, a uniform wetland removal rate, or a future nutrient load.
