# Phase 7B Assumptions

FACT: Dependencies are qualitative interfaces, not exposure magnitude, and generic receptors are contextual assumptions.[14][15]

FACT: Strong, moderate, limited, and unknown describe evidence strength only; unknown is not limited.[14][15]

FACT: Advisory, monitoring, treatment, and remediation records do not establish individual exposure, dose, or illness.[3][7][9]

FACT: The Toledo intake-coordinate discrepancy remains unresolved, and the package retains no project-established individual exposure or receptor-reaching plume at Luckey.[4][7][8]
