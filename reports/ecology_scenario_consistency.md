# Ecology Scenario Consistency

## Baseline separation

The factual Phase 6A ecology nodes, edges, indicators, and Map 20 remain unchanged. The validated Phase 6B dependency, disturbance, resilience, and Map 21 artifacts remain unchanged. Phase 6C uses separate scenario tables and separate maps.

## State coverage

All six scenario states are represented:

- A2050 and A2075 — Restored Connectivity
- B2050 and B2075 — Managed Working Basin
- C2050 and C2075 — High-Pressure Fragmented Basin

Each state carries six ecological components. Scenario assumptions, node states, edge states, disturbance states, resilience states, and comparison rows retain scenario/year fields.

## Interpretation

Scenario deltas express qualitative persistence, expansion, contraction, reconnection, fragmentation, management, monitoring, disturbance, and resilience changes. They do not imply linear progression, forecast certainty, probabilities, population change, or exact biological movement.

## Great Black Swamp

The historical candidate remains noncanonical. Scenario references to wetland restoration, drainage adaptation, and connectivity do not create a literal restored historical swamp boundary.
