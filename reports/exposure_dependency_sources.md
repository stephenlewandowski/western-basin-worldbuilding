# Phase 7B Sources

FACT: Phase 7B reuses the accepted Phase 7A source registry and its five pathway families.[12][14]

FACT: Claim-level records use NOAA and Ohio EPA HAB sources plus City of Toledo water-treatment information.[1][2][3]

FACT: Ambient-air context uses EPA AQS, Ohio EPA air monitoring, and the Michigan-relevant monitoring context retained by the Phase 7A package.[5][6][12]

FACT: Luckey control and groundwater context use USACE site and groundwater-monitoring records.[7][8]

FACT: Fish, recreational-water, and heat interfaces use the Ohio advisory/monitoring sources and NWS heat products.[9][10][11]

FACT: The package adds no new exposure inventory or quantitative health source.[14][15]
