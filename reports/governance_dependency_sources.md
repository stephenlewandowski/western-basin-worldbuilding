# Phase 10B Cross-System Governance Dependency Sources

The Phase 10B dependency and coordination registers reuse the Phase 10A source ledger at `data/processed/analysis/governance_sources.csv` and preserve row-level `source_id` provenance.

Water-quality, municipal-operation, USGS-observation, and Great Lakes research interfaces use Ohio EPA, City of Toledo, USGS, and NOAA GLERL sources.[2][3][4]
Additional supporting source records: [5][6][7].

The nutrient/agricultural chain distinguishes H2Ohio and NRCS assistance from regulatory permit obligations.[11][12][13]

Federal/state wetland, flood, and environmental interfaces use USACE, USFWS, FEMA, Ohio EPA, and Michigan EGLE program sources.[5][7][9]
Additional supporting source records: [35][36][38].

The binational and tribal coordination rows use the Great Lakes Commission, Governors & Premiers, GLWQA, IJC, GLIFWC, EPA Tribal, and nation-specific sources. The registry keeps advisory, treaty/agreement, consultation, and current-jurisdiction uncertainty separate.[14][15][16]
Additional supporting source records: [17][18][19].
Additional supporting source records: [20][21].

Energy dependencies use FERC, NERC, PJM, PUCO, FirstEnergy, DOE, and NRC context. Freight dependencies use FHWA, FRA, MARAD, the Port Authority, Ohio port law, ODOT, and private operator context.[22][24][25]
Additional supporting source records: [26][27][28].
Additional supporting source records: [29][30][31].
Additional supporting source records: [32][33][34].
Additional supporting source records: [40][42][43].

Public-health and warning dependencies use NWS, FEMA, Ohio Department of Health, and local-health context.[36][37][39]
Additional supporting source records: [41].

Coordination mechanisms are not presumed legally binding. `mandatory_or_voluntary`, `documented_or_inferred`, `binding_character`, and `evidence_strength` are separate fields.
