# Energy System Future Worldbuilding

## Boundary

The 2026 record is factual. Phase 3A's 18-node/17-edge baseline and Phase 3B's
5 generalized dependency nodes/28 dependency edges are accepted and frozen.
2050 and 2075 are six qualitative scenario deltas, not forecasts. The scenario
layer contains no power flow, feeder, route, facility-load, outage-probability,
reserve-margin, N-1, or restoration-time analysis.

## 2026 fact

The Western Basin's named energy system includes nuclear, gas, coal, renewable,
storage, regional-interface, water-treatment, wastewater, advanced-materials,
lime-processing, and planned/unverified compute objects. Monroe is operating
with a planned-retirement transition. Oppidan Bowling Green is
planned/unverified. Phase 3B identifies qualitative electricity, fuel,
cooling/water, weather, storage-support, and communications dependencies.

## Scenario interpretation

The three scenario families are intentionally non-ranked:

- **Managed Transition** emphasizes evolutionary replacement, efficiency, and
  continued regional coordination.
- **Distributed Resilience** emphasizes local flexibility, finite storage,
  critical-load support, and water-system resilience.
- **High-Load Convergence** emphasizes strategic materials, industrial
  electrification, technical manufacturing, and generalized compute growth.

Possible worldbuilding themes include major-generation retirement as a civic
transition, water security beneath energy policy, strategic industry competing
for reliable power, distributed resilience versus centralized efficiency,
storage limitations, compute as a physical industrial load, and energy
infrastructure as a political/security asset. These are fictional
interpretations, not 2026 findings.

## Interfaces carried forward

The scenarios retain the Phase 3B distinction between grid electricity and the
critical water/materials/compute functions it supports. Efficiency, flexible
demand, storage support, water reuse, industrial electrification, and
communications coordination are represented as generalized functions. They are
not assigned precise facilities, quantities, customers, or transmission paths.

## Open uncertainty

Nuclear license decisions, Monroe's retirement implementation, gas supply and
fuel logistics, storage duration and state of charge, cooling-water constraints,
industrial investment, compute commissioning, and local grid topology remain
open. Later Data/Sensors/Governance/Security work must not be smuggled into this
physical energy/load layer.
