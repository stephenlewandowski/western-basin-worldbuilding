## Source hierarchy and conclusions

USACE site and regulatory material governs the Luckey chronology. NIOSH, DOE,
OSHA, and ATSDR supply corroboration or general health/control context. The
research located no authoritative GIS contamination boundary appropriate for
this atlas, no documented individual Luckey exposure record suitable for a
finding, and no documentary basis for a direct Luckey-to-Elmore production
flow. Accordingly Map 09 uses authoritative facility points and qualified
text/schematic relationships.
