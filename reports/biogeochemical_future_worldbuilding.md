# Biogeochemical Futures — Worldbuilding Implications

These are speculative interpretations, separate from scientific claims:

- Nutrients become infrastructure: drainage, treatment, wetlands, soils, and sensors are managed as a basin metabolism.
- Phosphorus recovery and circular fertilizer systems create new strategic material and governance interfaces.
- Wetlands become treatment landscapes whose public value is negotiated season by season.
- Agricultural drainage becomes visible regional infrastructure rather than background property detail.
- Sensor-driven watershed management ties public trust to seasonal water conditions and the legibility of responsibility.
- Conflicts emerge over who bears responsibility for a material that moves across farms, jurisdictions, tributaries, and the lake.

A/B/C states are qualitative alternatives, not predictions.
