# Phase 13C Infectious Disease System Futures QA

The package contains 36 assumptions, 36 transmission states, 36 surveillance states, 36 response states, 36 dependency states, 36 uncertainty states, 33 scenario sources, six comparison rows, Maps 43/43b, and a qualitative comparison figure.[1][21][33]

The Python and independent R validators check schemas, exact row counts, unique IDs, scenario/horizon coverage, baseline node/dependency/uncertainty references, source references, assumption references, qualitative-only states, scenario/fact separation, substantive 2050/2075 differences, Map 43/43b raster/SVG integrity, prior freeze integrity, and all active holds.[23][30][31]

Negative-scope QA rejects future incidence or case-count forecasts, outbreak probability, disease-burden or individual-risk products, vulnerability/EJ scoring, unsupported local downscaling, surveillance = incidence, climate/vector suitability = disease, contamination = illness, infrastructure stress = illness, mobility = outbreak certainty, and Phase 14 implementation. Caveat language is retained as boundary evidence rather than treated as a positive claim.[2][7][3]

Maps are generalized system-state diagrams. They show archetype opportunity, surveillance/detection, response coordination, environmental/system dependencies, and uncertainty; they do not show future case totals, incidence surfaces, neighborhood disease risk, facility burden, outbreak hotspots, or individual exposure.[24][33][23]

Great Black Swamp remains C — HOLD / noncanonical. The Toledo intake-coordinate discrepancy remains UNRESOLVED. Deferred maintenance remains unchanged, and no Phase 14 implementation, release, or tag is included.[29][26][25]
