# Biogeochemical Scenario Consistency\n\nThe six scenario states share the same eight baseline component references and differ only through explicitly labeled qualitative deltas. Scenario A strengthens retention, soil function, restoration, adaptive management, and monitoring without eliminating HAB possibility. Scenario B retains a productive working landscape with selective controls and persistent managed export; it is not a numeric midpoint. Scenario C increases episodic mobilization, transport variability, management burden, and uncertainty without asserting collapse.\n\nAll scenario node, edge, control, and uncertainty records carry scenario identifiers, years, fictional status, assumption references, source identifiers, and qualitative states. No future quantity is combined with a 2026 observation or target. Climate appears only as an external driver.\n\nThe held Great Black Swamp candidate and unresolved Toledo intake-coordinate issue are not modified by this module.\n
