# Ecological Dependency Sources

Baseline: 2026

Phase 6B reuses the accepted/frozen Phase 6A ecological skeleton and its public sources. No new species inventory or sensitive occurrence dataset was acquired.

| source_id | dependency use | limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `usgs_nhdplus_hr`, `usgs_3dhp_all` | Lake, bay, river, tributary, floodplain, and hydrologic-connectivity structure. | Physical hydrography is not an ecological movement or passage model. |
| `usfws_nwi` | Wetland habitat dependency and wetland-alteration context. | Inventory does not measure condition, loss rate, or ecological redundancy. |
| `phase6a_usfws_ottawa_nwr` | Protected coastal habitat and migratory stopover support. | Generalized public context; no sensitive occurrences or exact routes. |
| `phase6a_glfc_lake_erie_committee` | Lake Erie fish-community and lake-to-tributary connectivity context. | No population, stock, spawning-site, or passage estimate. |
| `noaa_hab`, `epa_lake_erie` | HAB, nutrient, and aquatic-condition disturbance interfaces. | No probability, causal effect size, or risk score. |
| `h2ohio_great_black_swamp_history` | Historical drainage transformation and modern agricultural-matrix legacy. | The held Great Black Swamp candidate remains noncanonical and is not used as a boundary. |
| `phase6a_mrlc_land_cover` | Generalized terrestrial, developed-edge, and landscape-fragmentation context. | No large raster or habitat-quality metric is committed. |

All dependency, disturbance, and resilience claims are qualitative and source-qualified. Presence of a pressure does not establish a quantified ecological consequence.
