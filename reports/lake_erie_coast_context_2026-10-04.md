## Energy, water and heat

The [NRC applicant environmental report, §3.1.3](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/operating/licensing/renewal/applications/davis-besse/davis-besse-enviro.pdf)
describes Davis-Besse's closed-cycle natural-draft cooling tower and Lake Erie
makeup water; the [historical EIA Ohio profile](https://www.eia.gov/nuclear/state/ohio/index.php)
corroborates the configuration. Closed-cycle cooling still has withdrawal,
consumption and discharge relationships. It is not a claim of no lake impact.
The report is source context, not a new measurement of present cooling flows.

Thermal/cooling ecology, fish habitat and radiological monitoring are separate
questions. [NRC plant-specific effluent and environmental reports](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/operating/ops-experience/tritium/plant-specific-reports/davi)
ground monitoring work; their existence establishes no invented harm or absence
of harm. [EPA cooling-water intake context](https://www.epa.gov/cooling-water-intakes/cooling-water-intakes-final-2014-rule-existing-electric-generating-plants-and)
explains why aquatic organisms and cooling-water infrastructure need separate
attention.

The public numerical example uses a **hypothetical 10 MW facility load**, not IT
load with an unstated efficiency multiplier: 10 MW × 24 h = 240 MWh. Most consumed
electricity ultimately becomes heat. This does not establish cooling-water
quantity, heat-recovery temperature, usable recovered heat, annual utilization,
reactor allocation or a connection to a real center. Heat recovery needs a
compatible user, temperature, distance and equipment. Coaster regenerative
braking can recover a fraction of supplied energy; energy conservation prevents
it from being a net source for the park.

## Nuclear stewardship and alternative futures

[NRC high-level-waste guidance](https://www.nrc.gov/facilities-safety/high-level-waste)
distinguishes storage from disposal; spent-fuel pools and dry storage are interim
management, and reprocessing leaves waste needing disposition. An NRC
[Davis-Besse ISFSI inspection plan, 2024–25](https://adamswebsearch2.nrc.gov/webSearch2/main.jsp?AccessionNumber=ML24059A420)
provides site-specific storage context. Do not infer an announced regional
repository or a present local recycling process.

[DOE's advanced nuclear fuel recycling program](https://www.energy.gov/ne/advanced-nuclear-fuel-recycling-program)
records 2026 development activity. Fuel recovery remains a possible pathway,
with residual waste and separate licensing, disposition and community decisions.
The [2023 DOE consent-based siting framework](https://www.energy.gov/ne/us-department-energy-consent-based-siting-process-federal-consolidated-interim-storage-spent)
is dated context for community participation, not proof of an implemented
destination. No single technology is presented as a complete waste solution.

[DOE's fusion roadmap](https://www.energy.gov/fusion/office-fusion) addresses
remaining scientific and technical gaps toward pilot electricity; its milestones
are goals, not demonstrated dependable commercial supply or a Lake Erie siting
decision. [NRC fusion FAQs](https://www.nrc.gov/materials/fusion/faq) distinguish
tritium and activated-material handling from fission spent fuel. Fusion does not
dispose of inherited fission waste, and Davis-Besse is not depicted as a reactor
that can be converted into fusion equipment.

The three 2075 alternatives are **provisional design concepts**:

- **Successor fission:** new licensed generation and grid/compute relationships,
  potentially including modular designs, beside earlier retirement and custody.
- **Fusion joins:** a distinct new installation, conditional on dependable
  technology, fuel/materials handling, siting, grid connection and maintenance.
- **Custody outlasts generation:** old generation retires while storage,
  inspection, records and eventual lawful transfers continue; supply comes from
  another mix.

These alternatives are not ranked or assigned dates, capacities or probabilities.
The frozen EIA snapshot's 894 MW Davis-Besse capacity and planned/unverified
5 MW Bowling Green compute record remain unchanged. Nameplate capacity is not
generation, an agreement is not a physical delivery route, and an illustration
is not engineering evidence.

## How it enters the world

The Atlas page now places the service terrace inside a broader living region.
Separate chapters cover power/compute, waste stewardship, working security,
fish/migration and future entertainment. One scrollable connection chart follows
grid-mediated electricity, heat, cooling and custody as different relationships.
It adds no physical feeder route or quantified flow.

The imagined dusk encounter pairs a park electrician, refuge worker, fish-dock
crew and compute scheduler. It is a scene seed, not a shared real control room
or feeder. Future ride assemblies, shelters, seasonal shuttles and stewardship
rooms are plausible inventions rather than accepted canon. Existing artwork
still depicts unspecified utility rooms and a civilian environmental survey
craft, not a reactor, military platform, refuge or Cedar Point installation.
