# Ecology Future Worldbuilding

The scenario layer supports several interpretive themes without converting them into factual claims.

- Restored wetlands can function as ecological infrastructure and contested public investment.
- Ecological corridors can cross political, agricultural, industrial, and regional boundaries.
- Working landscapes can remain managed ecosystems rather than a simple nature-versus-development binary.
- Refugia and protected areas can become institutions of biodiversity continuity.
- Engineered ecological functions may coexist uneasily with self-organizing habitat.
- Connectivity can become a governance problem involving land access, maintenance, and authority.
- Monitoring and adaptive management can shape how uncertainty is publicly negotiated.
- Industrial heritage landscapes may become ecological mosaics without erasing their working history.

These are speculative worldbuilding implications of the alternative scenarios. They are not present-day vulnerability findings, forecasts, or predictions of ecological collapse.
