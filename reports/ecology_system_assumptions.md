# Ecology System Assumptions

Baseline: **2026**

Phase 6A is an ecological-system skeleton, not a complete species inventory, population model, conservation-priority ranking, or ecological-risk assessment.

## Components

The six overlapping components are:

1. western Lake Erie aquatic system;
2. Maumee / tributary / floodplain system;
3. coastal wetlands and marshes;
4. Black Swamp legacy and modern agricultural matrix;
5. terrestrial habitat and fragmentation context; and
6. migratory / mobile-species functions.

They are analytical layers and may overlap. They are not exclusive polygons or jurisdictions.

## Relationship semantics

Edges describe hydrologic, habitat, wetland, riparian, migration, spawning, restoration, or condition interfaces. A migration link is a broad ecological function, not an exact animal travel route. `fragmented_by` records a generalized developed-edge context only; proximity is not proof of causation or harm.

## Spatial policy

Existing accepted water, wetland, lake, and physical-hydrography layers are reused. Broad ecological systems retain blank coordinates. Ottawa National Wildlife Refuge and Maumee Bay State Park are generalized public anchors, not sensitive occurrence locations. The Great Black Swamp historical candidate remains **C — HOLD / noncanonical** and is not used as modern habitat geometry.

## Indicator policy

The three indicators are directly supported inventory counts: 608 generalized NWI wetland features, 864 existing physical Lower Maumee flowlines, and seven contributing HUC-8 watersheds. They are context indicators, not species richness, abundance, habitat-quality, or vulnerability metrics.

## Exclusions

No sensitive rare-species locations, nests, roosts, dens, population estimates, complete insect inventory, exact migration route, fish-passage engineering model, vector-disease ecology, contamination attribution, ecological-risk score, future ecological scenario, or climate projection is included.
