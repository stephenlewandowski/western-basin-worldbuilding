# Phase 7A Findings

## Drinking Water / HAB
Western Lake Erie source-water/HAB condition, monitoring, Toledo intake context, treatment, and generic water-system users form a supported chain. Source-water condition is not finished-water condition, monitoring is not exposure, and no cyanotoxin dose is inferred. The Toledo intake-coordinate discrepancy remains unresolved.

## Ambient Air
EPA AQS/AirData and Ohio/Michigan monitoring provide a modest ambient context for supported criteria pollutants. The package does not assign concentrations to facilities, draw plumes, infer inhaled dose, or infer illness or causation from proximity.

## Luckey
Phase 2C evidence supports documented legacy contamination, groundwater/soil monitoring, remediation, dust/water controls, and institutional control context. It does not establish individual exposure, a receptor-reaching plume, attributed illness, or a regional contaminated-sites inventory.

## Fish and recreational water
Ohio fish-advisory and recreational-water monitoring systems provide generic advisory pathways. Actual fish consumption or recreation is not assumed, and advisories are not documented harm.

## Heat
NWS observation and warning products provide broad 2026 heat/humidity stress context for the general public and outdoor workers. No personal dose, heat illness, mortality, future climate scenario, or urban heat-island simulation is modeled.

Across all pathways, no record demonstrates an actual individual exposure relationship. The product is exposure context, not disease analysis.
