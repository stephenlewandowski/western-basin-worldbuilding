# Phase 7A Assumptions and Boundaries

- This is a factual 2026 context layer, not an exposure assessment, toxicological inventory, epidemiology study, or risk assessment.
- Potential pathway means a plausible environmental interface supported by the cited evidence; it does not mean documented individual exposure.
- Generic receptors are analytical categories, not identified people, residences, workplaces, or patient groups.
- Ambient monitoring points are not interpolated into plumes, exposure zones, or neighborhood scores.
- Source-water condition and finished-water condition are separate records.
- Luckey contamination and remediation are reused from Phase 2C; no plume or receptor-reaching pathway is created.
- Fish and recreational advisories do not establish use, consumption, dose, or harm.
- Heat observations and warnings do not establish personal heat dose or illness.
- Strong monitoring is not high exposure; limited monitoring is not high risk.
- Great Black Swamp remains C — HOLD / noncanonical; the Toledo intake discrepancy remains unresolved.
- Vector/infectious disease, environmental justice, cumulative exposure scoring, future health scenarios, and Phase 7B are excluded.
