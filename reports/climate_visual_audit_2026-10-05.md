# Atlas season and thermal-condition audit

**5 October 2026 · 21 distinct displayed raster assets visually inspected**

Scope: eleven futurescape/Hall scene images, five equipment/crib ink sketches,
the illustrated regional map and four preserved research plates. Farm and
Industrial Study covers reuse lead images and are not double-counted. The
withdrawn comic was not used. Audit performed by a read-only visual-review agent.

**Disposition for every asset: keep unchanged; amend future art direction only.**
Most scenes suggest ordinary temperate working weather, not unmistakable severe
summer heat. This is a portfolio gap, not grounds to invalidate or relabel the
images. No image establishes numerical temperature, heat-wave status, water
temperature, ice trend, air pollution or cooling performance. Visible vents,
plants, glazing and pipes must not be renamed as proven thermal systems.

## Portfolio priorities

1. Glass City: shaded passage versus hard sun, and one visible consequence when
   shade is interrupted. Keep solar-control performance conditional.
2. Warm night: occupied shade/refuge, difficult recovery, late work and ordinary
   family life, without an automatic dramatic sunset or cyberpunk glow.
3. Bio-Ag: alternate dry/heat work with the preserved wet-gate scenes. Make
   drainage versus conservation a choice, not uniformly brown dying fields.
4. Coast: a depth-marked recorder or fictional profile artifact whose status is
   explicit; variable winter and summer conditions, no water-color diagnosis.
5. Industry/Hall: distinguish heat-producing rooms, conditioned occupied space,
   filtration and service access spatially. Respect existing Hall orientation.
