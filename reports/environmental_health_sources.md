# Phase 7A Environmental Health Sources

The baseline uses a small, authoritative source set: City of Toledo water-treatment information; Ohio EPA HAB, air, fish-advisory, and recreational-water programs; EPA AQS/AirData; Michigan EGLE air monitoring; NOAA/GLERL HAB science; NWS heat products; and USACE Luckey remediation and groundwater-monitoring records. Source IDs and URLs are recorded in `metadata/sources.yml`.

Evidence classes are kept separate: environmental evidence describes a condition or contaminant; monitoring/control evidence describes observation, treatment, remediation, regulation, or advisory; exposure evidence would require an actual person-level or otherwise authoritative exposure demonstration; health-outcome evidence would require a separate health study. Phase 7A contains no documented individual exposure or health-outcome relationship.

The Toledo intake-coordinate discrepancy is preserved as unresolved. Existing accepted Water, Information, Ecology, and Phase 2C Luckey baselines are reused without modifying their artifacts.
