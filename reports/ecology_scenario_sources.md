# Ecology Scenario Sources

Phase 6C is a qualitative scenario layer over the frozen factual Phase 6A and validated Phase 6B ecological baselines. It reuses their public sources and does not perform an exhaustive future-driver literature review.

| source group | scenario use | limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NOAA / EPA HAB and Lake Erie sources | Aquatic condition, nutrient, and HAB scenario drivers. | No future bloom probability, abundance, or effect size. |
| USFWS NWI and Ottawa refuge context | Wetland function, protected support, and migratory habitat scenarios. | No future preserve boundary or sensitive occurrence. |
| USGS hydrography and watershed sources | Hydrologic and riparian connectivity scenarios. | No future flow, passage, or route simulation. |
| H2Ohio / Ohio DNR Great Black Swamp history | Historical drainage legacy and working-basin scenario context. | The held geometry is not a restoration boundary. |
| MRLC land-cover portal | Generalized terrestrial and agricultural landscape-driver context. | No future land-cover raster or conversion forecast. |
| Great Lakes Fishery Commission | Fish-movement and aquatic-management scenario plausibility. | No population trajectory, stock forecast, or precise spawning location. |

Scenario assumptions are interpretive alternatives, not forecasts or probabilities.
