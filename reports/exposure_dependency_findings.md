# Phase 7B Findings

Drinking Water / HAB depends on source-water condition, monitoring, treatment, and generic water-system users; source water is not finished water and HAB observation is not human exposure. Ambient Air has measurement, state interpretation, and generic inhalation dependencies, but no plume, facility contribution, or dose. Luckey has documented contamination, monitoring, remediation, and land-use-control interfaces without individual exposure or illness attribution. Food/Fish/Recreation depends on advisories and behavior; an advisory is not proof of consumption or harm. Heat depends on meteorological observation, warnings, and generic outdoor context; no personal dose or illness model is made.

The project can support environmental presence, potential pathways, documented monitoring/control, and evidence gaps. It cannot support documented individual exposure, dose, disease, causation, cumulative risk, or exposure magnitude.
