# Phase 7B Findings

FACT: Drinking Water / HAB uses source-water condition, monitoring, treatment, and generic water-system-user interfaces; source water is not finished water and HAB observation is not human exposure.[1][2][3]

FACT: Ambient Air has measurement, state interpretation, and generic inhalation dependencies, but the package assigns no plume, facility contribution, or dose.[5][6][15]

FACT: Luckey has documented contamination, monitoring, remediation, and land-use-control interfaces without an individual exposure or illness attribution.[7][8][15]

FACT: Food/Fish/Recreation depends on advisories and behavior; an advisory is not proof of consumption or harm.[9][10]

FACT: Heat depends on meteorological observation, warnings, and generic outdoor context; no personal dose or illness model is made.[11][15]

INFERENCE: The project can support environmental presence, potential pathways, documented monitoring/control, and evidence gaps.[14][15]

INFERENCE: The package cannot support documented individual exposure, dose, disease, causation, cumulative risk, or exposure magnitude.[14][15]
