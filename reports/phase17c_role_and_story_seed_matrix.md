E = evidence/model basis; S = scenario condition; K = narrative/character sketch; C = canon. Every new role, relationship, institutional arrangement, personal pressure, and thread here is K, including those built on an S packet. No character validates a future condition. A/B/C regime variations remain open; no single future is selected. No probabilities, exact sites, invented intervening historical events, or new factual claims are introduced.

PLACE → SYSTEM → ROLE → ROUTINE → DEPENDENCY → PRESSURE → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE governs the set. The water works, drainage landscape, industrial interfaces, cooling access, sampling chain, inspection practice, and compute room remain legible without these particular people. Other staff, owners, laboratories, and authorities exist outside the selected viewpoints; this is not a complete institutional cast.

## Human-scale and diversity check

Three primary non-controller viewpoints are ROLE-04 (resident/caregiver), ROLE-08 (small operator/customer of larger systems), and ROLE-10 (contract worker/commuter). ROLE-03 and ROLE-06 also expose the worker's side of authority rather than acting as system owners. ROLE-04 has very little formal authority; ROLE-06 has high technical competence but no asset-isolation authority; ROLE-09 has formal delegated authority but incomplete technical knowledge.

Life-stage options remain K and deliberately light: early-career ROLE-05, established specialist ROLE-06, care-intensive household ROLE-04, and late-career ROLE-10. No exact ages or biographies are fixed. Security ranges from utility employment to hourly wages, contract work and a small enterprise's narrow cash buffer. Trust can be procedural for ROLE-01, contingent on responsive notices for ROLE-04, and grounded in field checks for ROLE-06; none implies a moral ranking. Automation reliance differs between advisory comparison, inspection tools, manual scheduling and printed room hours. Spare-part access differs between a utility request chain, a small farm and a restricted facility. These contrasts are prompts to test, not demographic explanations of ideology.

### NT-01 Two Correct Records

Place: Lake Erie Energy & Security Coast / Glass City Core; generalized shoreline utility rooms, lake wind outside weathered concrete.
Roles involved: ROLE-01, ROLE-06; relief staff and authorized utility communicators remain outside the seed cast.
Systems involved: Water, observation, maintenance, governance. Lineage: FT-01 (S), FT-11 (K).
Status: K

Ordinary starting condition: A shift operator prepares a routine handover while a contractor completes an approved comparison of an advisory flag with local records.
Trigger: An observation summary and a treatment log appear inconsistent just as the contractor's permitted visit is ending.
What is initially known: Both records have provenance. Their time windows and points of observation differ; neither has been shown defective. The operator can continue documenting the distinction.
What remains uncertain: Whether a comparable observation would narrow the disagreement, and who can authorize the necessary follow-up within the remaining access window.
Conflicting obligations: The operator owes the next shift usable information, not an invented answer. The contractor owes a bounded report and has another paid appointment. Public communication requires its own authorized review.
Decision point: Request an expanded comparison, or close the visit with a specific unresolved question and an assigned handoff rather than an all-clear.
Possible consequence paths: Further access could delay the contractor's next customer. A narrower handoff could preserve the uncertainty but require another shift to repeat coordination. Neither path establishes deteriorating treatment.
Physical artifacts involved: Time-stamped sample record, manual logbook, signed work order, local gauge and a damp access badge.
Why this exposes the world: The technology can work normally while different valid observations resist one answer. Maintenance time and evidential responsibility cross an institutional boundary.
Non-claims: Observation is not control; disagreement is not treatment failure; contamination, exposure, dose and illness remain distinct. No future facility layout or health instruction is specified.

### NT-02 The Visit That No Longer Fits

Place: Maumee River Commons / Black Swamp Country interpretive interface; field entrance, ditch marker, wetland edge and repair yard.
Roles involved: ROLE-02, ROLE-05, ROLE-08; other land operators retain separate choices.
Systems involved: Drainage, agriculture, ecology, sampling and repair logistics. Lineage: FT-02 and FT-10 (both S).
Status: K

Ordinary starting condition: A gate-part delivery and an authorized sampling visit share an agreed access period. The steward has left room in the work calendar for both.
Trigger: Wet weather narrows the usable entrance window without damaging a gate, vehicle or instrument. The next available delivery appointment overlaps the sampler's laboratory handoff.
What is initially known: The part is available, the sample equipment works, and the access agreement has a stated scope. Neither visitor can treat that agreement as permission to use another entrance.
What remains uncertain: Conditions beyond the inspected reach, another land operator's willingness to accommodate access, and whether a repeat visit would remain method-comparable.
Conflicting obligations: The steward needs field access and must honor the shared drainage arrangement. The sampler must protect method and custody. The haulier cannot reserve an unpaid day against every possible weather change.
Decision point: Separate the visits at an explicit cost, seek a revised agreement, or defer one task while recording the resulting limitation.
Possible consequence paths: A separate delivery could consume the steward's repair reserve. A deferred sample could leave a qualified gap rather than an invented trend. A negotiated visit could shift another operator's work, without demonstrating harm or benefit downstream.
Physical artifacts involved: Gate fitting, muddy culvert marker, custody form, cooler, booking sheet and handwritten access note.
Why this exposes the world: Fully functioning tools do not supply consent, dry ground or shared calendars. The landscape remains productive and inhabited rather than becoming a problem built for a hero.
Non-claims: No held Great Black Swamp geometry, parcel identity, crop outcome, nutrient-removal result, incidence or guaranteed drainage consequence is asserted.

### NT-03 A Recoverable Lot Without a Receiving Standard

Place: Great Lakes Industrial Belt; tagged storage beside an old loading bay, with rail vibration and patched pavement.
Roles involved: ROLE-03, ROLE-08; the buyer, laboratory and data owner remain separate off-matrix participants.
Systems involved: Materials, freight and data stewardship. Lineage: FT-03 (S).
Status: K

Ordinary starting condition: A segregated residual is being assessed for recovery. The quality worker has reserved a receiving slot conditionally, not certified the material for use.
Trigger: The buyer asks for a comparison under a receiving specification that the available characterization does not cover. A vehicle booking is about to become chargeable.
What is initially known: Existing measurements are valid within their scope. Recoverability does not settle qualification for this process. The haulier has another booking, and the record-serving equipment is operating.
What remains uncertain: Whether a scoped retest would satisfy the buyer and whether the off-matrix data owner permits disclosure of the needed lot record. Neither selected role can grant that permission.
Conflicting obligations: The quality worker must preserve traceability while production wants the storage space. The haulier needs an honest loading decision. The separate data owner must review the disclosure request within its authorized scope.
Decision point: Hold the lot, ask for a specific retest and cancel the booking, or negotiate a later receiving slot while keeping the stream visibly unqualified.
Possible consequence paths: Storage work and expense may increase. An external input could meet a process need but create a new loading task. A successful retest could still meet an unavailable buyer; no branch guarantees sale or use.
Physical artifacts involved: Sample jar, segregation tag, calibration sheet, receiving specification, booking docket and access-request record.
Why this exposes the world: Normal measurement and compute operation leave standards, ownership and timing unresolved. The district remains connected to outside markets and labor even when recovery is technically conceivable.
Non-claims: Residual is not automatically resource or qualified input. No exact process, throughput, profitability, closed loop or new operational network is established.

### NT-04 The Room Opens After the Shift Starts

Place: Glass City Core; retrofitted brick building, cooling-room doorway and a short route whose shade ends at exposed pavement.
Roles involved: ROLE-04, ROLE-09, ROLE-10; the household member, employer and building operator are not collapsed into these roles.
Systems involved: Heat adaptation, settlement, paid labor, access and public information. Lineage: FT-04 (S).
Status: K

Ordinary starting condition: A caregiver plans a household member's visit around posted room hours and the start of a paid shift. Cooling equipment is functioning and the opening schedule was accurately published.
Trigger: The household's accompaniment arrangement changes. The room's staffed opening is now too late for the caregiver to make the shift without another agreement.
What is initially known: The cleaner is present before opening but has neither admissions responsibility nor paid cover for public occupancy. The coordinator can negotiate staffing and approve program hours, not certify technical readiness alone.
What remains uncertain: Whether paid cover can be arranged, whether the household member wants the alternative, and whether another option actually fits the day.
Conflicting obligations: The caregiver wants companionship not reduced to logistics, and needs wages. The cleaner owes a defined task, not free supervision. The coordinator must make promises that staffing and the building operator can support.
Decision point: Seek a paid schedule change or revised opening, accept lost work time, or decline this room option without pretending another is equivalent.
Possible consequence paths: An extension could move cleaning or staffing costs elsewhere. Waiting could consume wages and rest. A refusal could prompt a better notice of access limits without solving this household's day.
Physical artifacts involved: Printed hours, cleaning checklist, keys, water bottle, work roster and a notice correction slip.
Why this exposes the world: Nothing needs to malfunction. Infrastructure provision, staffed access and usable household time are different achievements, negotiated by people with legitimate limits.
Non-claims: Adaptation does not eliminate heat stress. No individual exposure, illness, neighborhood coverage, safety guarantee or fixed future public-service entitlement is asserted.

### NT-05 A Signal With Its Method Attached

Place: Maumee River Commons / Black Swamp Country interpretive interface; wetland access edge, field vehicle and an authorized environmental/public-health notice channel.
Roles involved: ROLE-05 is the selected viewpoint; an off-matrix authorized environmental/public-health communications or review office is the counterpart. The sampler cannot interpret laboratory findings or clear publication.
Systems involved: Ecology, surveillance, data stewardship, governance and communication. Lineage: FT-10 (S).
Status: K

Ordinary starting condition: The sampler submits a labeled observation through routine custody. The off-matrix office prepares an aggregate update, not a clinical advisory.
Trigger: A qualified detection arrives beside a previous non-detection collected with different effort. A request for a simple comparison arrives before the notice deadline.
What is initially known: Both records retain their methods and time windows. The laboratory has qualified what the new test detects, not a population claim. Neither observation needs to be wrong.
What remains uncertain: Comparability, local attribution and the meaning of the difference for any wider population. More compute cannot supply an absent denominator or standardize sampling.
Conflicting obligations: The sampler wants limitations preserved without losing the observation. The authorized office owes timely and intelligible information, but must obtain appropriate clearance and avoid releasing restricted detail. Silence and simplification impose different costs.
Decision point: Seek clearance for a bounded observation notice with method caveats, or postpone the comparison while explaining exactly which interpretation is not yet supported.
Possible consequence paths: A limited notice could generate questions the program must staff. A delayed comparison could leave residents seeking clarification through other channels. A repeat visit could improve comparability while displacing another route; none establishes an outbreak or absence of disease.
Physical artifacts involved: Cooler label, effort log, custody sheet, laboratory qualification, notice proof and method-caveat insert.
Why this exposes the world: Fully functioning diagnostics produce obligations as well as information. Useful communication is work, not a dashboard's automatic conclusion.
Non-claims: Detection is not population burden; surveillance signal is not incidence; presence is not transmission; infection is not clinical disease. Non-detection does not establish absence, and this seed chooses no pathogen or local disease event.

### NT-06 The Authorized Hour

Place: Great Lakes Industrial Belt / Glass City Core interface; compute-room maintenance door and a warm corridor in a reused building.
Roles involved: ROLE-07, ROLE-10; workload owner, access authorizer and cleaning employer remain distinct off-matrix actors.
Systems involved: Compute, energy/cooling, building service, labor and authorization. Lineage: FT-12 (K), FT-04 (S) for the cleaner's separate shared-space obligation.
Status: K

Ordinary starting condition: Planned filter service is scheduled between workloads. The technician has compatible parts and a tested local-record procedure. The cleaner's corridor task fits the original window before another building assignment.
Trigger: The workload owner requests an extension while the entry authorization still covers only the original period. No hardware failure or malicious lockout is required.
What is initially known: Compute and cooling are operating. The requested timing change crosses separate workload, building-access and paid-roster decisions. A recommendation can identify spare compute time without authorizing anyone's entry or overtime.
What remains uncertain: Whether the parties can approve a revised window, what work can be moved, and whether the next maintenance opportunity fits the relevant procedures.
Conflicting obligations: The technician owes properly authorized maintenance and wants the shift to end. The cleaner owes another client work and refuses to make unpaid waiting invisible. The workload owner has a legitimate completion commitment but cannot settle every building obligation.
Decision point: Request a jointly approved revision with explicit paid cover, or retain the original service plan and ask the workload owner to choose an authorized alternative.
Possible consequence paths: A revised window could cost another client a cleaning slot. A task could wait or use bounded local records, increasing clerical work. A deferred service request could remain in the maintenance queue for review, not become an automatic outage.
Physical artifacts involved: Filter carton, access log, work permit, key ledger, roster and printed fallback procedure.
Why this exposes the world: Working capacity is not available authority or free labor. Institutional limits are physically encountered at a door.
Non-claims: No capacity, redundancy, site, security topology, automatic dispatch or guaranteed continuity is specified. Software does not acquire civic authority.
