# Phase 17B — Future Translation Seed Matrix

Status: **S/K scenario-development material for human review**. Entry / seed translation set: **ACCEPTED FOR DEVELOPMENT**. No row is canon.

This is a first representative set, not a complete system-by-region matrix. It translates accepted current systems, dependency logic, technology-convergence states, Phase 16A propagation rules, and the four accepted Phase 16B principal stress tests into material future conditions. No new scientific model, quantity, probability, ranking, forecast, or external source is introduced.

## Reading rules

- `E` identifies the evidence/model anchor only. The future condition itself is `S` (scenario) or `K` (working sketch).
- `ROBUST ACROSS A/B/C` means the translation proposition survives all three regime envelopes without implying equal deployment or success.
- `REGIME-SENSITIVE` means the condition depends materially on the institutional, technological, workforce, maintenance, or legitimacy arrangements described by A/B/C.
- `UNRESOLVED` means accepted material leaves an important physical or institutional choice open; it is not a low-probability label.
- Black Swamp Country is used as an interpretive macroregion. No Great Black Swamp candidate geometry is used or promoted.

## Coverage summary

The 15 units represent all five macroregions and intentionally use overlapping interpretive regions rather than treating them as jurisdictions or mutually exclusive polygons. They cover all 13 Atlas systems through cross-system combinations, with agriculture, working waterfronts, wetlands, drainage, and industrial reuse represented as place and function rather than as new ontology members.

System coverage includes water/hydrology; nutrients/biogeochemistry; climate/natural hazards; energy/grid/compute; data/sensors/decision; governance/jurisdiction; freight/industry/material flows; materials; ecology/biodiversity; exposure/environmental health; population/settlement/mobility; vector ecology; and infectious disease. Technology convergence appears through sensing, AI/advanced compute, automation, defensive cyber, storage/distributed energy, advanced materials/manufacturing, molecular monitoring, privacy/data governance, and human-maintained interfaces.

Envelope count: **5 ROBUST ACROSS A/B/C**, **7 REGIME-SENSITIVE**, **3 UNRESOLVED**. These counts describe the translation disposition of the seed units, not relative plausibility.

## Source gaps retained for human review

The accepted repository supports qualitative translation but does not support exact 2050/2075 deployment, adoption, capacity, throughput, route, facility configuration, service territory, material performance, or successful-response claims. The Phase 15 technology layer describes capability and interfaces, not universal basin implementation. The accepted Phase 16B package freezes four principal qualitative compound tests; reserve candidates 005 and 006 are not used as executed future conditions here. Exact farm parcels, industrial facilities, future retrofit dimensions, local inspection fleets, future disease incidence/transmission, exposure, dose, illness, and Great Black Swamp historical geometry remain outside this seed transaction.

## Future-condition cards for human review

The cards below are short, concrete tests of the translation grammar. They are not ranked, are not “best” or “most likely,” and do not establish canon.

### FT-01 Water at the Intake

Place: Glass City Core / Lake Erie Energy & Security Coast
Systems: Water, energy, data, governance, climate
Status: S

2026 anchor: The water system is linked to energy, observation, and governance interfaces. The physical Toledo intake crib is a distinct verified asset; nearby monitoring entities are not the crib.

2050 transition: Selected sensors, communications, load-shifting routines, conservation options, and manual fallback procedures gather around the existing system.

2075 condition: The water works is a layered place of old concrete, steel, pipes, cabinets, sample bottles, and observation interfaces. River, lake, intake, treatment, and weather readings arrive from different sources and are compared rather than simply obeyed. An advisory model flags a change; a person checks the record, calls another institution, and decides whether to alter a routine, collect a sample, or issue a conditional notice. Some data arrive late or disagree. The system has not vanished into a seamless network.

Human-scale expression: Operators keep paper procedures, walk inspection routes, and explain uncertainty. People hear a pump, watch lake light move across a window, and receive a notice that says what is observed without promising control.

Visible artifacts: Tagged sample coolers, weathered railings, sealed sensor cabinets, work orders, screens, binders, and a manual log beside a digital display.

Maintenance / failure signature: Probe cleaning, cable replacement, battery changes, winter access, sensor drift, and delayed coordination. Observation is denser; authority, exposure, dose, illness, and successful treatment remain separate.

Uncertainty: Future coverage, capacity, adoption, facility configuration, and health outcomes are unresolved.

Status: S
