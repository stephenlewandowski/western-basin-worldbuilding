# Ecology Scenario Assumptions

2026 is the factual baseline. 2050 and 2075 are qualitative scenario horizons. No scenario is a forecast and no probabilities are assigned.

## A — Restored Connectivity

Wetland and riparian restoration, nutrient management, protected-area linkage, ecological monitoring, and adaptive management expand. Land availability, coordination, maintenance, and uncertain ecological response remain constraints.

## B — Managed Working Basin

Agriculture, industry, cities, transportation, and ecological functions continue together through targeted buffers, drainage adaptation, selective restoration, and management. Fragmentation persists without treating this scenario as a simple midpoint between A and C.

## C — High-Pressure Fragmented Basin

Compound ecological pressures intensify faster than broad restoration and connectivity improvements. Refugia, management intensity, and uncertainty increase; ecological collapse and extinction are not assumed.

## Scenario-data policy

Scenario nodes and edges remain separate from `ecology_system_nodes.csv`, `ecology_system_edges.csv`, Phase 6B dependencies, disturbances, and resilience tables. Future records carry scenario ID, year, assumption, source/basis, plausibility, and explicit fictional status. The Great Black Swamp candidate remains held and noncanonical.

No future abundance, richness, population trajectory, extinction, exact range shift, precise sensitive location, vector/disease process, or probability is modeled.
