# The Usual Table

**Glass City · provisional interactive fiction · July 2075**

Leena brings six peaches and dominoes for her first Friday afternoon off in three
weeks. Jo brings the cushion. A removed glass-roof panel puts their usual public
table in the sun. Their friends are coming; they need somewhere to spend the
afternoon, rather than an explanation of the canopy.

The playable [scene module](../src/atlas/glass-city-summer.ts) holds the complete
opening and three mutually exclusive endings. The same Glass City page keeps
its original lead/detail/sketch and adds this separate summer scene at `#summer-scene`.

| Choice | What changes | What resolves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Longer shaded public route | Time and location | Friends meet at a new table; start late, keep the peach |
| Market room | Privacy and company | A shared indoor table; one game and unexpected company |
| Ask for the shop's shade | Permission and company | Shopkeeper grants this afternoon's use and joins the game |

There is no prescribed best ending, danger, illness, countdown, score or saved
state. Restart clears the choice. No optional investigations or extra dashboard
are needed: the opening makes all three possibilities legible.

## Scene / thermal assignment

- Composite Toledo-derived river-edge street; hot, hard-sun July afternoon,
  little wind. No numerical air temperature or pollutant condition is assigned.
- One cassette is already absent; work stays behind barriers. Public passage
  skirts the bay. Nobody enters an elevated service route or passes beneath a lift.
- Leisure clothing is lightweight; workers retain task-appropriate protection.
  A refill bottle, cushion, fruit and shade boundary make heat an ordinary condition.
- The shop's table requires specific permission. The illustration does not
  establish its service connections, surveyed property geometry or a permanent
  public right. The broader futurescape's established unconnected neighbor is unchanged.
- Arcade, market room, characters and events are provisional fiction. Neither
  fan nor shade establishes cooling performance or guaranteed public refuge.
- Image shows the table encounter, not a sequence of panels. Generated figures
  do not establish portrait canon. The withdrawn comic remains withdrawn.
