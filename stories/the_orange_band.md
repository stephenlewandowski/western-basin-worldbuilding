## A scene worth entering

Toledo, a hot July afternoon in 2075. Renata’s daughter and other children are
waiting beside the side channel behind their building. Renata is at the Basin
Reading Hall, needing a yes-or-no answer about wading.

The Water Conditions Card is a short summary for one place and period. Blue dots
mark direct field visits; an orange model band extends interpretation beyond
them; gray means this card supplies neither direct observation nor a supported
model result for that location. None means safe, unsafe, clean or contaminated.
Their side channel is in gray, just beyond the orange band.

The central artifact is deliberately inspectable before deciding, rather than
an unexplained map mentioned in dialogue. It has a compact permanent legend and
a larger native-dialog card: fictional Willow Steps side channel, 12–14 July
2075, edition 4, a schematic map, three blue observation points, an orange model
band and a gray/hatched side channel. A ring marks the place Renata means; it is
a story annotation, outside the public edition, not an observation or a real
address. **SCHEMATIC / NOT TO SCALE** is visible.
The gray channel and enlarged location ring are emphasized on opening. Readers
can tap the blue dots, orange band or gray channel, or use three native buttons,
to reveal a one-sentence explanation. This changes emphasis, not the record.
The invented dates, route and interpretation record are story material, not
monitoring/GIS data or output from the current Model Lab.

## Questions and choice

- **What does the orange mean?** The printed edge looks like a clearance boundary;
  Renata asks what that representation actually says.
- **Where did the field crew actually go?** After receiving staff take Hannah’s
  closed case, a dry route copy at a public table distinguishes the field route
  from the model band: main channel to the crossing, then back, without entering
  their side channel.
- **Check the exact channel.** Renata gives permission to use the photograph’s
  location attachment to confirm they mean the same bend. This private comparison
  supplies no water answer and adds nothing to the public card.

Before the choice, her daughter sends another message: “We brought the old towels.
Mara’s already taken her shoes off.” It adds an ordinary child’s preparation and
peer pressure without changing the evidence or introducing a real-time timer.

Questions can be reread before deciding; the card can be opened and closed at
any time without affecting progress. There are three equal, unscored choices:

1. **Not tonight:** tell her to stay out. The afternoon is lost; nothing proves
   the precaution necessary. A wording-review request supplies no new measurement.
2. **The place they meant:** ask her to wait for an exact-location check, then
   go to her. If the optional comparison already happened, the ending acknowledges
   the same point and the choice becomes “Ask her to wait for you.” A private
   review note promises neither field visit nor answer. Her daughter is angry;
   Renata sits beside her.
3. **A line becomes a promise:** tell her the orange stops before them. The kids
   go in; no sensational event follows. Later, the daughter treats Renata’s
   bounded-map statement as permission. The conversation stops there.

The third ending introduces its own wording-review request before referring
to it later. It is never an approved new edition or added observation. The
precautionary ending says “This card doesn’t cover our channel,” avoiding a
claim that nobody ever visited it in any period. No ending establishes what
the water is like or an officially correct decision.

Prior inspection changes Renata’s wording only. Opening the card lets her mention
gray/no answer; asking Hannah about the physical route lets her mention Hannah’s
visit, even if another conversation is read afterward. Field-route wording takes
precedence when both were inspected. Each third-ending message still says “The
orange band stops before you. Stay on our side,” preserving the misunderstanding.
No consequence or choice is unlocked by attention. Restart clears both memories;
card inspection remains separate from the exact-location comparison.

## Continuity and information boundaries

[Orientation A](../docs/phase_briefs/hf05_reading_hall_continuity_lock.md) remains
unchanged: counter front-left, reading floor open, instrument spine right,
field return/custody threshold left-rear, service circulation behind the rooms.
Renata stays on the public side. The closed case crosses only to receiving
staff; a dry route-record copy reaches the public table after that handoff.

The steward distinguishes a visited point from modeled interpretation and can
match a fictional location privately. These are provisional institutional
arrangements, not known future jurisdiction or universal monitoring capability.
The Hall neither decides for Renata nor manufactures evidence.

Project practices inspire provenance, separate observations/models, explicit
limits, private/public boundaries and wording review. No real empirical or
synthetic Model Lab result is used by the fictional card. No scientific products,
Waterville record, accepted model outputs or canon register change.
