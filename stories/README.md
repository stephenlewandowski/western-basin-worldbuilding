# Stories from the Western Basin

The Atlas supplies places; stories let people live in them. These are provisional
fiction rooted in the basin’s geography and the project’s existing systems work.
Science belongs in the grounding records; a scene needs a person with somewhere to go.

Start with **[Before the Last Bus](https://stephenlewandowski.github.io/western-basin-worldbuilding/atlas/basin-reading-hall/#story)**,
a short interactive scene in the Basin Reading Hall. Renata has a question, a transit
card and her daughter’s paper sun. Three conversations lead to different ways of
carrying the question home. [Scene card and comic beats](before_the_last_bus.md).

Six older ordinary-day scenes are preserved in the
[worktree draft archive](../docs/archive/README.md):

| Person | Draft | Life beyond the system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nadine | An ordinary utility shift | Bicycles, bowling and a Sunday meal |
| Dale | Farm work and an access gate | The pancake-breakfast urn |
| Adam | Industrial qualification | A cover band |
| Renata | The Hours on the Glass | Resale shirts, a cardigan, a daughter’s bean plant |
| Hannah | The Second Visit | A departing roommate and an ugly green bowl |
| Wendell | Waterfront service | An outboard engine |

These drafts are sources for adaptation, not a selected canonical cast. The original
Hannah draft has a duplicated opening sentence; preserve it in the archive and remove
that duplication in a future reading edition. No new character biography is settled here.
