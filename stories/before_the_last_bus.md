# Before the Last Bus

**Format:** a short illustrated, choice-based scene; provisional fiction.
**Place:** the developing Basin Reading Hall, composite Toledo, 2075.
**Read/play:** [Atlas scene](https://stephenlewandowski.github.io/western-basin-worldbuilding/atlas/basin-reading-hall/#story).
The exact published dialogue and endings live in [the scene module](../src/atlas/reading-hall.ts).

Renata wants to know whether a finding includes the little channel behind her
building, and still get home before the evening disappears. Hannah returns from
fieldwork; the steward keeps old editions beside the current one. Behind the staff
rail, a technician’s instrument trolley has a stubborn cheap wheel.

Renata can inspect the earlier edition, ask Hannah what the visit covered, or look
at the unavailable instrument compartment. Each conversation offers a different
piece of context. She can request a clearer public scope note, leave a detailed
location privately, or catch the bus with the question unfinished. Public wording,
private detail and follow-up time produce different consequences. None yields an
instant answer about water or automatically changes a finding.

## Six comic beats

1. Wide hall: the daughter’s paper sun peeks out of Renata’s bag; two editions
   share a cabinet. The ground service trolley sits behind a staff rail.
2. Hands: a steward lays two dated folders on worn timber. “Its label was too
   easy to read as a promise.”
3. Threshold: Hannah wrestles a closed cooler over a lip. “I can tell you where
   I went. And where the van couldn’t go.”
4. Detail: the small instrument cabinet, the fixed room and the offending wheel.
   “Every expensive machine eventually meets a cheap wheel.”
5. Choice: Renata folds a location slip or asks for public wording; a receipt says
   pending. An empty slip remains available if she leaves.
6. Bus: the paper sun wears a receipt for a hat. In the private-request ending,
   Hannah and Renata discuss the ugly green bowl; in the no-request ending,
   Renata photographs a resale shirt in the window light.

The figures in the composition studies illustrate the space; they are not locked
portraits of these characters. A comic adaptation should establish a consistent cast.

## Grounding and reuse

Renata and Hannah derive from the existing
[cast prototypes](../reports/phase17c_recurring_cast_prototypes.md) and preserved
ordinary-day drafts. Their names, family details and this encounter remain fiction.
Model Lab’s saved runs and source checks inspire the hall’s record practices;
the [production brief](../docs/phase_briefs/phase17d_hf05_basin_reading_hall_production_brief.md)
supplies its access and maintenance arrangement. The public editions, privacy
handoff and civic office are invented institutional arrangements.

The cabinet, comparison dock, service berth, paper sun and unresolved channel
question can recur. Test them in another place before assigning a new invention
catalog number. The next story can begin with the return visit rather than a new
world-explanation document.
